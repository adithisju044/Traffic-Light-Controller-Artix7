TRAFFIC LIGHT CONTROLLER - EXPECTED SEQUENCE

1. North/South GREEN, East/West RED  (GREEN_SEC)
2. North/South YELLOW, East/West RED (YELLOW_SEC)
3. North/South RED, East/West RED    (CLEAR_SEC)
4. North/South RED, East/West GREEN  (GREEN_SEC)
5. North/South RED, East/West YELLOW (YELLOW_SEC)
6. North/South RED, East/West RED    (CLEAR_SEC)
7. Repeat at phase 1.

Defaults: 100 MHz clock, 10 s green, 3 s yellow, 1 s all-red clearance.
